`survivor.glb` is a web derivative of `characterMedium.fbx`, baked in its idle pose.
The FBX control rig was removed to avoid skeletal transform incompatibilities.
The two original survivor textures are retained. The shared GLB is approximately
208 KiB and is loaded once and reused for all players, including mobile devices.
Movement, orientation, hit reactions and combat effects are driven by the arena.
Procedural backpack and weapon accessories were added by this project.
